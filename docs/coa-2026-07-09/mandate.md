# Calendar of Activities and Actions (CBD) - v2

## 1. Overview
The **"Calendar of Activities and Actions"** (often referred to simply as the **"Calendar of Activities"**) is an online administrative and operational tool developed by the Secretariat of the Convention on Biological Diversity (SCBD). It was created under the guidance of the Bureau of the Conference of the Parties (COP) to rationalize intersessional activities and facilitate workflow management.

## 2. Exact COP Decision Mandating the Calendar
The formal establishment and mandate for the "Calendar of Activities and Actions" comes from recent decisions aimed at improving efficiency, specifically:

*   **Decision CBD/COP/DEC/16/25 (adopted in 2024 at COP 16 in Cali, Colombia):** 
    Titled *"Options to further improve the effectiveness of processes under the Convention and its Protocols,"* this decision explicitly requested the Secretariat to provide national focal points, at the end of each year (starting in 2024), with a **"calendar of activities and actions"** expected in the following year. 
*   **Purpose of the Mandate:** The stated goal in the decision is to help rationalize intersessional activities, facilitate workflow management, and ensure Parties have clear expectations for upcoming meetings, document submissions, and peer reviews.
*   **Historical Context:** This builds upon previous decisions, notably **Decision 15/18** and **Decision XII/29**, which continuously sought to streamline agendas, facilitate early preparations for meetings, and avoid duplication of efforts between governing bodies (COP) and subsidiary bodies (SBSTTA and SBI).

## 3. Knowledge Domain & Ontology: "Action" vs. "Activity"
Within the decisions adopted by the COP to the CBD, terms like "activity" and "action" are rarely given strict dictionary definitions. However, from a knowledge mapping and ontology perspective, they follow a very clear hierarchical structure that turns high-level treaties into ground-level results:

*   **The Mandate (The "Why"):** The COP Decision. This is the legal or authoritative basis.
*   **The Action (The "What"):** The strategic projects, plans, and steps required to fulfill the COP decision. This represents the substantive compliance work or policy commitment (e.g., National Biodiversity Strategies and Action Plans, or submitting a specific compliance report).
*   **The Activity (The "How" & "When"):** The logistical events, milestones, and workshops. This is the vehicle on a timeline used to execute the Action (e.g., official meetings of the COP, Subsidiary Bodies, or Ad Hoc Technical Expert Groups).

**In Summary:** An **Action** is the strategic project, plan, or step required to fulfill a COP decision. An **Activity** is the specific event, milestone, or task on a timeline that executes that Action.

## 4. How the SCBD Tool Represents this Reality
The underlying data architecture of the SCBD's online tool was explicitly built to reflect this ontology, merging the *timeline of Activities* with the *compliance checklist of Actions*:

*   **The Anchor is the Activity (Time-based):** Because it is a *calendar*, the primary objects displayed are Activities (e.g., an upcoming Ad Hoc Technical Expert Group meeting). They have fixed start and end dates. 
*   **The Metadata is the Action (Task-based):** The tool specifically includes filters for **"Action required."** This means the SCBD database links specific compliance steps (like submitting a peer review or nominating a scientist) to the chronological activities. 
*   **The Traceability to the Mandate:** The tool allows users to filter by "Global Biodiversity Framework (GBF) sections & targets," directly linking the ground-level Activity and Action all the way back up to the COP Decision (the Mandate).
